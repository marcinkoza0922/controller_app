# On-screen menus

A menu is a list or ring of choices that appears on screen. Choosing an item presses its action, such as a key or a macro. You build menus on the setup's **Menus** tab.

## Opening one

Use **Open menu…** as the action of any button, gesture, combo, trigger or stick direction. The menu stays up while you hold that input, and closes when you let go.

## Keeping one open

Wrap **Open menu…** in **Toggle**. The menu then stays up until you press the input again.

## Choosing

Move with the left stick or D-pad, and press A to choose. B goes back one level, or closes the menu at the top. Some items have a quick button, which you can press to choose them without moving.

> **Tip:** a list with more than eight rows is shown in two columns.
