# Your first change

1. Plug in your controller and open the **Overview** page. It appears in the controller list, and the picture follows your presses.
2. Press **Test rumble** to feel the vibration, if your controller has it.
3. Open **General**, or one of your setups, and go to the **Profiles** tab.
4. Click a button's name to open its editor. Each mapping shows a one-line summary, such as `A ▸ Left click`.
5. Not sure which button is which? Press **Find by pressing**, then press the button on your controller.
6. Press **Save & apply**. The change starts working straight away.

## Start from the defaults

General comes with two profiles:

- **Gamepad** passes your controller straight through, as if padwight weren't there.
- **Desktop** moves the mouse with the left stick, scrolls with the right stick, makes A and B click, and sends Alt+Tab with LB and RB.

> **Tip:** **Save & apply** is only active when something has changed.
