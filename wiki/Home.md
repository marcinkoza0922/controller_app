# padwight

padwight is a background service that remaps gamepad inputs, or turns them into mouse and keyboard input, with a settings window for editing profiles. It creates virtual devices (an Xbox 360-style pad, a keyboard and a mouse) and switches profiles automatically when a game gets focus.
